Version 2.2.9:
==============

- The game texts can be shown in English or German, the language can be changed
  in the options and is applied immediately. Thanks to kadrim!
- The multiplayer host also sees the map size and the random seed. Thanks to kadrim!
- The AI keeps fewer knights in the castle (limited by the free knights, at least 3
  if there are more than 3, always 1), builds a weaponsmith in hard times if there
  is steel, releases the knights of needless military buildings if it has no free
  knights (only if no land is lost), builds military buildings at the growing
  border and splits roads at congested flags (e.g. at the castle)
- Fixed the emergency program blocking all construction sites forever when the
  stones ran out although the essential buildings didn't need any. The AI could
  not expand anymore then.
- Fixed mines which got no food anymore although there was plenty of it
- Fixed flags drawn in front of buildings after scrolling the map
- Fixed roads and other sprites cut off at the top and left edge of the view
- Amiga graphics: the map tiles and roads are displayed properly now
- Better performance with large settlements
- A special click on the build button toggles the possible building sites again
  (it could only be switched on, not off)
- The building filter's third page shows the mill and the butcher at their
  original positions (they were swapped, so the mill was hidden behind the
  butcher)
- Android: the app now supports Android 15+/16 devices with 16 KB memory pages.
  The Silk.NET SDL native libraries (which were only 4 KB-aligned) are replaced
  with 16 KB-aligned builds for arm64-v8a and x86_64, removing the XA0141
  build warning and the load failure on 16 KB-page devices.


Version 2.2.8:
==============

- A special click (left + right button, long press on Android) works as in the
  original: on a build button it shows the possible building sites, on the map
  button it shows the castle, and only a special click demolishes. In the map box
  a special click on the buildings button opens the building filter (Bld1-4) with
  the original's building pictures; the minimap then shows only the chosen
  building type. Ported from freeserf PR #562.
- The game decides a winner and shows the end of the game with the original's
  pictures and texts: the result picture, the result text with the winner's face,
  and after a won mission the mission's picture. A click closes the box and asks
  whether to quit. Ported from freeserf PR #573 and #574.
- The tutorial mode with the original's six tutorials: the game type can be
  selected in the game init box, each tutorial has its own map, goals and castle
  supplies, and the tutorial orders box shows the current goal. Tutorial 6 adds
  a passive enemy. Ported from freeserf PR #575, #576 and #577.
- Save games keep the game type, the mission/tutorial level and the winning player
- Thanks to dos-ise for the special click, the end of the game and the tutorials!
- Fish only spawn in shallow water next to the shore, so lakes can be overfished:
  2-3 fishers can share a lake of the size of a screen, a 4th one overfishes it (#47)
- Mines also mine the ground below buildings and flags (#158)
- Fixed loading games in which a castle was destroyed (#142)
- Fixed doubled serf counts after loading (#139)
- Fixed a crash with a wrong castle knight count (#130)
- The possible building sites are updated while they are shown (#131)
- Fixed the direction (#138) and position (#152) of the resource chart
- The scroll wheel no longer turns a chart into the minimap (#128)
- Sounds of serfs and buildings outside of the screen are no longer played (#129)
- Parts of a burning mine no longer stay on the screen (#127)
- Serfs waiting inside a building are no longer drawn in front of it
- Linux: the audio libraries are found on more distributions (#87)

- Fixed a deadlock of serfs waiting for each other (loop check at flags)
- Fixed no knights being made while a Knight0 is in the stock
- Fixed transporter bits being cleared at a crowded flag
- Fixed the military score of the statistics (overflow and wrong formula)
- Fixed pigs oinking too often (persistent random generator)
- Fixed the DOS smelter sound (clip 62 offset and padding)

  - the burning counter of buildings

    the timers, the messages and the statistics history of the players
  - the knight morale and inventory schedule counters of the game
  - the threat levels of the military buildings (not computed again)
  - the serfs leaving a stock
  Older save games load as before.
